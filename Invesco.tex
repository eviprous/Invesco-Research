\begin{block}{Motivation}

Traditional \textbf{capital-weighted portfolios (CW)} often lead to concentrated exposures, 
where a few large-cap stocks dominate overall portfolio risk. 
This imbalance raises the question of whether alternative weighting schemes can achieve 
better \textbf{risk efficiency} and \textbf{return stability} without sacrificing exposure to market factors. 

The \textbf{Equal Beta Contribution (EBC)} approach provides a systematic way to equalize 
risk contributions across assets, aiming for more balanced beta exposures 
and improved portfolio diversification. 

This study evaluates how EBC compares to CW and \textbf{Equal-Weighted (EW)} portfolios 
in terms of \textbf{Sharpe ratio, volatility, and factor-adjusted performance}, 
using data from the S\&P 500 universe.

\end{block}


\begin{block}{Data Description}
\begin{itemize}
  \item S\&P 500 constituent returns (1960–2025)
  \item S\&P 500 Market Caps (1960–2025)
  \item FF5 + Momentum factors (Kenneth French Library)
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Hypothesis}
\textit{H\textsubscript{0}}: EBC portfolios do not outperform CW portfolios in risk-adjusted terms.  
\textit{H\textsubscript{1}}: EBC portfolios yield higher Sharpe ratios and lower systematic beta exposure.
\end{block}

\end{column}

\begin{block}{Methodology}
Weights under the Equal Beta Contribution framework are defined as:
\[
w_i = \frac{\frac{1}{\beta_i}}{\sum_j \frac{1}{\beta_j}}
\]

We compute:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Rolling monthly betas via CAPM regression
  \item Cross-sectional Fama–MacBeth regressions
  \item HAC-adjusted t-statistics for factor loadings
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Empirical Model}
\[
R_{p,t} - R_{f,t} = \alpha + \beta_{MKT}(R_{MKT,t} - R_{f,t}) + \sum_{k}\beta_k F_{k,t} + \varepsilon_t
\]

Factors include MKT–RF, SMB, HML, RMW, CMA, and MOM.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Implementation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Python + Pandas for data pipeline
  \item Statsmodels for regression
  \item Rolling window estimation: 12-month overlapping
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\end{column}

\begin{column}{\colwidth}

\begin{block}{Key Results}
\begin{itemize}
  \item EBC portfolios exhibit an average annualized Sharpe ratio of 1.32 vs 1.07 (CW)
  \item Lower volatility: σ(EBC) = 0.87 × σ(CW)
  \item α\_annualized = 2.1\% (t = 2.45, HAC-corrected)
\end{itemize}

\begin{alertblock}{Conclusion}
\begin{itemize}
  \item EBC outperforms CW in both mean-variance efficiency and downside risk.
  \item Factor loadings reveal lower market beta and smaller exposure to size and value factors.
  \item Next step: Bayesian shrinkage on β estimates and multi-factor risk decomposition.
\end{itemize}
\end{alertblock}
